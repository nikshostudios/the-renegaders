The customer gives us almost nothing

Camera · Full-screen face

Here is the entire customer brief: “I need a wallet.”

That is it.

No brand, no material, no colour, no price. And somewhere inside fifty thousand products is the one they actually want.

So rather than tell you our agent understands shopping intent, here is the whole conversation running end to end.

CameraStart on your face. The deck can be full screen behind you or appear immediately after this line.
DeliverySay the customer request plainly. Pause after “I need a wallet,” then let “That is it” land on its own.
ThenMove to Shot 2 immediately after “running end to end.”

The released public baseline

Screen · Presentation Shot 2

Before I run it, one number, just so you know where we started.

The official starter found the target in twenty five out of two hundred released public sessions. Twelve and a half percent.

3

Run the real conversation

Screen · Full terminal · No face bubble

Switch to the terminal and run: python3 demo.py --top-k 3

Alright. So this is the first message: “I’m looking for wallets, but I’m still exploring.”

It is a decent start. The matches are wallets, and it asks what requirement matters most.

But it still does not know the material or the colour. The customer has not said either of them yet. Which is exactly why it asks.

So this is what I’m going to give it: leather, black.

That is it. Two words.

And there we go. Black leather wallets have moved to the top.

Notice what I did not repeat. I never said wallet again.

The first message gave it the shopping job. The second message gave it the judgment. The agent kept both.

ScreenKeep the terminal large enough that product titles and IDs are readable. Type the two customer turns exactly.
Do not claimDo not call any displayed recommendation the hidden target. This live run is not scored against hidden ground truth.
ThenReturn to Presentation Shot 4 only after the second recommendation list is visible.
4

Four small mechanisms

Screen · Presentation Shot 4 · Face optional

Now, underneath that, four small things are happening.

It keeps the useful words from earlier. It asks for the missing detail. It drops filler like “what matters is.” Then it reranks the same catalog.

A match in the product title counts more than a loose mention in the description.

And the same conversation gives the same result every time. There is no model call behind it.

The customer never sees any of that. They just do not have to start again.

AnimateUse “Run the flow” on the deck so the four mechanisms appear one at a time.
Plain languageDo not speak SQLite FTS5, BM25, or field-weight jargon. The slide may show technical labels quietly.
ThenMove to the result as soon as the customer consequence is clear.
5

The complete public evaluation

Screen · Presentation Shot 5

Alright, that is one conversation. So then we ran all two hundred released public sessions.

The starter found twenty five targets. This agent found one hundred and eighty seven.

That gives us a Hit Rate at 10 of 0.935, a technical score of 0.808740, and an average of 2.945 turns to reach the target.

We tried other changes along the way. Some sounded clever and made the score worse, so they did not make it into this agent.

PointMove from 25 to 187, then Hit Rate at 10, technical score, and average turns.
EvidenceThese are the released public sessions. Private-set performance remains unknown.
OptionalIf you show a fresh full evaluator run, disclose that it takes roughly 28 seconds. Otherwise show the checked result file.
6

Separate the result from the claim

Camera · Full-screen face

Now, the demo and the evidence are two different things, so here is exactly what we can claim.

Zero model tokens. Zero API cost. No network call.

On one local run, the median was twenty two milliseconds. On a later run it was forty six, so that number depends on the machine and its load.

These are public development results. We have not seen the private set.

What we can prove is that the complete public result reproduced exactly.

The customer added two words, and the agent knew what those two words belonged to. That is the whole thing.

CameraFinish on your face. The proof slide can sit behind you, but your delivery is the final image.
HonestyDo not turn public development evidence into a private-set claim. Keep the host-dependent timing qualification.
EndStop after “That is the whole thing.” Do not add an improvised promise or claim.

Do not drift

  • Never state or imply a private-set result.
  • Never call a live recommendation the correct hidden target.
  • Do not show catalog contents beyond normal product titles in the agent output.
  • Do not show an absolute machine path, credential, private message, or unrelated project.
  • If a take contains a wrong number, rerecord it instead of correcting it in captions.
